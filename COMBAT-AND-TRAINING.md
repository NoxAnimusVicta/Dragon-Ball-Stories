## Learning & Mastery

Credits = actual productive hours × training quality. Quality ranges from 0.25 to 25. A fully supported twelve-hour programme at quality 25 earns 300 credits; sixteen productive hours earn 400. There is no eight-hour credit cap. Sleep, meals and inactive breaks are not simultaneous practice.

Learning is below 10 credits; Learned starts at 10, Proficient at 250, Advanced at 2,500 and Mastered at 10,000. Thresholds promote automatically without an exam or promotion roll. Established feats remain valid; historical Developing/Practised labels do not automatically assign numerical tiers.

Intensity, feedback, challenge, live application, environment, recovery support, attention, transferable understanding and review contribute to quality. Fatigue, interfering injury, poor matching, technical error, distraction and hostile conditions reduce it. Advantages add rather than multiplying into a mandatory checklist. Many kinds of practice can develop the same skill.

Primary practice earns hours × quality. Supporting skills can receive 50%, 25% or 10% for strong, secondary or incidental involvement, totalling no more than 100% of primary work. No recursive credit. New combinations have 40 setup credits; original inventions 120; an established method has none. Later stages of an existing line do not repeat invention.

Before Learned, each genuine eligible combat execution with novice failure risk earns1 learning unit after resolution, successful or failed, capped at10. Use pre-attempt progress for the failure draw and a unique attempt ID to prevent repeated awards. Declarations, pellets and repeated ledger reads are not extra attempts. After Learned, ordinary combat use grants no automatic proficiency; dedicated practice including focused sparring still earns hours × quality, without double-counting the same work.

Training does not raise a transformation’s fixed multiplier. It improves activation, control, energy use and duration. Learning a new state still requires its recorded prerequisites. Reliable deliberate transformation combinations require mastery of the underlying methods and separate compatibility training; established feats and involuntary external access remain exceptions.

Mastered is the final profile at10,000 cumulative credits. Preserve earned totals as historical practice records, but additional credits grant no further cost, timing, damage, capacity or handling bonuses. Further development uses separately recorded prerequisite-gated techniques, forms or variants; surplus credits never automatically transfer or satisfy a new preparation requirement. This applies to player and NPC methods alike. Ordinary Power Level growth has a separate reviewed ledger; publishing this reference does not recalculate earlier gains.

Before Mastered, use the recorded progression policy for each field consistently in calculators, current capability and Path to Mastery. Form activation/upkeep and Yardrat energy-cost arrays use geometric interpolation between tier anchors; discrete timing, output/capacity, recovery, handling unlocks, ordinary-attack costs, named-attack economy, Kiai full-boost upkeep and Spirit Healing tissue-repair costs use their tier entries. Do not infer interpolation merely because a field has four values. New profiles must record their field policy before use. All progression stops at the Mastered entry.

## Tolerance & Recovery

Carry tolerance T, unrecovered strain D, highest attained tolerance H and maintenance gap N. T and H cannot exceed 20; passive tolerance loss stops at 5. Personal-best memory is retained. These are fictional campaign measures, not human sleep guidance.

Light, normal, hard and intensive practice create 0.25, 0.50, 0.75 and 1.00 load per productive hour. Recovery capacity C = T × min(1, recovery hours / 8) × (0.75 + 0.125 × support), where support is 0–2. Closing D = max(0, opening D + load − C). The next session’s quality deduction is min(4, D / 2). Do not count the same fatigue twice or erase it at midnight.

Use actual completed recovery, not a promised night’s sleep. Ordinary productive and protected recovery hours do not overlap; an explicitly restorative body has the exception below. A mixed programme may be easier to sustain than continuous intensive work; no compulsory physical/mental activity split is imposed.

Demanding effort E = sum(hours × intensity / 3), with intensity 0–3. Recovered adaptation budget a = 0.1 × E × min(1, C / (opening D + load)); no load or recovery gives no adaptation. First-time tolerance grows as sqrt(T² + a). Below the recorded personal best, only the portion rebuilding H gains four times that squared-growth budget; growth beyond H returns to the normal rate.

Maintenance anchors: T5 needs 0.5 effort per week with 30 unsupported grace days; T10 needs 2 with 14 days; T15 needs 7 with 7 days; T20 needs 14 with 2 days. Interpolate between anchors. Meaningful contested combat supplies 0.2 × intensity / 3 per exchange, capped at 2 daily maintenance units, without creating imaginary practice hours or counting a counter twice.

Use training effort plus only combat maintenance not already counted by its actual duration. Daily requirement M is weekly demand / 7. Coverage = activity / M. Update N = min(grace + 1, max(0, previous N + 1 − coverage)). Beyond grace, lose 0.05 × max(0, 1 − coverage) tolerance that day, with a floor of 5. Full maintenance stops loss; returning to a former peak is faster, not instant. Skills and base power are not removed by tolerance decay.

An embodied-dead Other World body or explicitly equivalent restorative effect recovers training strain at 0.50 + T / 80 per actual hour, including practice. During protected rest use the greater ordinary/passive rate for overlapping hours, never both. At T12, twenty-four hours recover 15.6: Normal training creates 12 load, Hard 18, Intensive 24. Normal work can therefore clear old strain while harder work can accumulate it. Actual hours still earn credits; T stays capped at 20. A living visitor or sleep-removal-only wish does not automatically gain this recovery. It does not heal every wound or refill all combat energy.


## Attacks, Defence & Counters

Physical Combat (legacy ID combat-basics, formerly Combat Fundamentals) covers ordinary punches, kicks and close-range strikes: a flurry of lighter blows, fewer heavy blows or one committed heavy strike use the same bounded-attempt profile. Ki Blasts (legacy ID ki-blast) covers ordinary unnamed shots, volleys, concentrated blasts and beams, including mouth-fired blasts where that fighter has the handling. These are narrative delivery variations, with identical damage, expenditure, multiplier and engagement accounting at the same skill tier; fewer blows do not add a heavy-attack bonus or divide the damage. Actual limbs, range and firing path still determine feasibility. Distinct methods such as Kamehameha, Special Beam Cannon and the recorded Break Cannon retain their own profiles; an ordinary mouth blast does not acquire those properties. Each attempt resolves once, never once per depicted hit or pellet.

Meaningfully contested combat uses automatic private, saved rolls for everyone. Fix intent, actual capabilities, supply, timing, range, injuries and feasible outcomes first. Impossible and certain results need no random draw. No rerolls, lucky minimum chance, protagonist padding or retrospective bonuses.

For attack aiming, use the Power Level available for that action, excluding a technique-bound bonus. With r the ratio, t=min(1,abs(ln r)/ln4), w=r^(0.5+1.5t²); r0 gives0. Apply independent agility², skill, position and directional familiarity once. Chance=w/(1+w). This retained close-gap curve reaches the squared curve at fourfold gaps. Damage still scales linearly with the Power Level during the technique.

After a failed clean breakthrough, a secondary draw chooses guard, dodge, parry or a counter opportunity. An interception response remains pending until collision. Neutral weights are 50/25/15/10. Trained rotations change that conditional distribution; they are not another chance to reverse a successful hit. Remove impossible responses before rolling and normalise the remaining weights.

A Counter transfers half the current attack segment under clock v2; ordinary Guard does not. The transferred interval is debited from its initiator and credited only to the responding fighter. It may fund a feasible response; an attack pays ordinary costs and uses ordinary accuracy. No separate free attack, whole new turn or duplicate global time.

Physical counters use a ready Physical signature or style-appropriate strikes; Ki counters use a suitable Ki signature or ordinary Ki Blast variation. A response never copies an unknown move or receives free charge. A feasible Ki interception can start a clash: test uncertain interception once, then compare actual delivered powers. Collision is not a free hit on the caster. A weaker response may be overrun.

Working convention: one signature attack per active form/loadout, with optional form overrides referencing already learned moves. Physical and Ki attacks qualify; Transformation & Amplification and Discipline do not. It is a default preference, not a limit on choosing other attacks. Zero defaults to Spirit Spear when ready and in contact range; ordinary physical combinations or Ki Blast volleys/spheres cover other windows. Spirit Bomb is not a reliable emergency signature, and a maximum-power basic blast gains no invented multiplier.

An exchange groups a tight combination or bounded rapid-blast volley, not damage per fist or pellet. One root combination and its immediate counter chain earns one readable familiarity/maintenance exposure; every actual attempted attack still costs endurance. Changed tactics, supply, positions or genuinely subsequent attacks create new windows.

## Recovery & Lasting Damage

Check each actual impact after Power Level scaling and resolved defence, before clipping it to remaining Endurance, applying passive regeneration or spending recovery reserves. At 100 or more Endurance damage, a target without true immortality dies immediately if the attack has killing intent; with nonlethal intent the target is immediately unconscious and incapacitated. Neither major nor full recovery can intervene. Fix intent before resolution. Cell/Buu-style regeneration alone is not true immortality. True immortality retains its recorded recovery, subject to the existing Zeno-level or specific reality-erasure exception. Separate impacts never pool toward100; expenditure and upkeep are not injury. Below100, an actually lethal wound can still kill. Ordinary zero alone remains exhaustion, not automatic unconsciousness or death.

Major partial recoveries, including Second Wind and Namekian-style repair, take 45 actual seconds at every tier and Power Level. Record start and completion deadline; restore the fixed amount only on completion. At zero the fighter cannot fight, maintained voluntary forms drop immediately and unsupported holds/preparations end. An ally can buy time; no protection prevents an enemy finishing the casualty. Full regeneration is immediate and can preserve an active state if it restores viable Endurance before collapse. Recovery has no Endurance cost.

Below the terminal-hit rule, apply an established passive injury refund once, then subtract loss. At zero use an available suitable full regeneration first and carry remaining damage through it immediately. Otherwise reserve one eligible major recovery for its45-second deadline and retain the unpaid overflow. At completion subtract that overflow from the fixed restore; if exhausted again, another available major recovery needs its own45seconds. Never discard excess or apply passive refund again. Further injury is resolved when it lands and its excess persists; it does not silently restart the deadline. Death, terminal incapacitation or loss of the actual regenerative mechanism cancels pending restoration. External healing that resolves the depletion cancels the unspent pending recovery without a duplicate restore; it does not reset spent slots.

Track Major injuries only. Lesser cuts, bruises and wear remain in Endurance and narration. Resolve contact/defence and calculate actual post-defence damage before clipping to current Endurance or applying a passive refund. A single resolved attack dealing50 or more produces one Major injury. One combination is one attack, not an injury check per punch. Separate impacts do not pool toward50; attack expenditure, movement and upkeep never trigger injury rolls. The existing100-damage terminal rule takes priority: nonimmortals die under killing intent or become unconscious/incapacitated under nonlethal intent before recovery. True immortality retains its established erasure exceptions. Below100 an actually unsurvivable wound can still kill; a pending recovery cannot postpone death.

Fix the attack's mechanism, targeting declaration, anatomy and feasible locations before the attack roll. After contact/damage are resolved, use the established contact location; otherwise draw torso40, head10, each arm15, each leg10, filtering unreachable/unexposed locations and normalising. Other anatomy needs an equivalent recorded distribution before resolution. Filter type weights by the actual mechanism and location, then normalise. Determinate anatomy needs no random draw. An arm cannot be lost from an isolated torso impact and an ordinary punch cannot cause a thermal burn.

## Stacking and treatment

Every distinct injury stacks when applicable: odds factors multiply, cost surcharges add, bleeding rates add, and functional restrictions accumulate. Two ×0.60 injuries give ×0.36; two +25% cost injuries give ×1.50; two bleeding wounds give6 Endurance/minute. For opposed positioning apply each participant's affected odds factor to that participant's weight before taking the ratio. There is no strongest-only limit or hidden penalty cap. A missing function makes the action unavailable rather than merely unlikely. Do not apply a generic extra Power Level reduction or penalise unaffected actions. Injury surcharges affect the stated actions, not form upkeep. A zero-cost action remains zero after a surcharge; actual bleeding remains separate.

One wound may have several consequences: a severed arm and its bleeding are one injury. One contiguous reconstruction caused by one impact can include several damaged structures, such as an extensive section of a bio-android's torso. Establish its repair grouping when it occurs, not afterward to reduce healing costs. Distinct wounds elsewhere and distinct later injuries remain separate. A missing arm blocks two-handed techniques unless the fighter already has a usable alternative; it does not disable kicks or mouth beams. Filter impossible defences before rolling.

Suitable first aid takes45 seconds and stops bleeding from one accessible wound without repairing it or restoring Endurance. Internal bleeding needs appropriate access/healing. Accrue each active bleeding rate over actual shared elapsed seconds, including recovery windows, once per wound; split at treatment, healing and depletion. Bleeding is damage over time, not a fresh50/100-damage impact or another injury roll. Keep actual injury functions and penalties until repaired; ordinary Second Wind never clears them.

Targeted repair can start above zero. Reserve all required uses at start, spend them at completion and start their existing cooldowns then; cancellation releases unspent reservations. Ongoing damage and costs still apply. At zero, targeted repair alone leaves the fighter exhausted. Regenerative biology replaces ordinary Second Wind rather than granting both allowances.

## Allies & Multiple Attackers

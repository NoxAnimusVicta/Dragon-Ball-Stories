# Narrative combat and retained training

The former numerical combat system was retired on 26 October 2026. Its complete source is preserved in the owner's private archive. It is not an active rules reference.

Use [Narrative Combat](NARRATIVE-COMBAT.md) for power-gap expectations, meaningful opposition and contextual consequences. No Endurance pools, attack-damage formula, fixed faction turns, action budgets, recovery charges or defensive-rotation skills remain active.

Training is retained: Learning below10, Learned10, Proficient250, Advanced2500 and Mastered10000 cumulative progress. Productive hours × assessed quality gives learning work. Intensity contributes once inside quality; maximum Intensive quality is25. Training displays duration, productive hours, intensity, quality and actual gains. Supporting practice and efficient redistribution at mastery remain governed by the private training owner. Mastered is terminal; new techniques require their own prerequisites and practice. Historical credits are preserved, including retired skills, without reallocating them into free gains.

The app keeps acquired techniques, progress, mastery handling, established multipliers and art. `combat-reference.json` is retained only as a compatibility filename for the training thresholds. It no longer contains combat rules. Private NPC records and future prerequisites are not published.

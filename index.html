ADVENTURE MENU

Made for the journey.

The game menu for your Dragon Ball Isekai. Your confirmed character details and enacted story events will live here. Unrecorded values remain unknown, never zero by assumption.

The Capsule Corp styling is a design theme; it doesn't establish Jake's affiliation or ownership of any equipment. The landscape is decorative menu scenery. Radar signals open menu sections; they are not story coordinates.

Controls

Use the arrow keys to move through the menus, Enter to select, and Escape to return to the main menu. Sound is off until you enable it. Use Motion to pause the animated scenery and radar.

Keep it close

Use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or app installation option, where available. An internet connection is needed for current records.

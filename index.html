ADVENTURE MENU

Made for the journey.

The game menu for your Dragon Ball Isekai. Explore Zero’s character, abilities, possessions and story. Details appear here as they are established during the adventure.

The landscape and Capsule Corp styling set the mood. The records below reflect the places, people and equipment established during play. Decorative menu scenery does not add story facts.

Controls

Use the arrow keys to move through the menus, Enter to select, and Escape to return to the main menu. Sound is off until you enable it. Use Motion to pause background animation. Your motion preference is remembered on this device.

On your phone

Use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or app installation option, where available. The app checks for updates when you open or return to it, and every minute while it is visible. New versions arrive automatically, preserving your screen and place. An open panel waits until you close it. An internet connection is needed; while offline, the screen already loaded stays available.

# Narrative combat reference

Adopted 26 October 2026. This replaces the mechanical combat system. It is a campaign judgement guide loosely informed by the former power-gap curve, not a canonical formula or a probability table.

## Compare the fighters who are actually fighting

Compare the two fighters' currently usable Power Levels. Apply the same guide to the protagonist and NPCs. Compare actual active states, not a locked form or a theoretical maximum. Use established fatigue, injury, mastery, tactics and special abilities when judging what can be sustained; there is no Endurance pool or numerical fatigue-to-power conversion.

Use the player's supplied domination chart as the campaign reference, not as a verified canonical formula. The ratio below is the stronger fighter divided by the weaker fighter; read both sides of the same row. These expectations supersede the initial 26 October broad power-gap table.

No injury quotas, automatic wins or immunity to surprise, a vulnerable state, specialised effects or a sufficiently amplified technique. Do not invent an exception merely to cancel a deserved advantage, or make an enemy incompetent to protect the protagonist. Apply the same expectations to both sides.

## Meaningful resistance and choices

Before resolving an exchange, establish what the enemy is trying to accomplish and how they respond to the observed threat. They can attack, guard, evade, interrupt, retreat, feint, grapple, exploit terrain, protect allies or escalate. Choose from their actual repertoire, personality, knowledge and condition. Pride can explain allowing a buildup; it is never a universal free pass. Characters can be beaten, captured, seriously injured or killed when the circumstances justify it. No protagonist immunity and no invented hard counter to punish creativity.

Repeated attacks meet an opponent who remembers them. Describe changes in angles, rhythm, distance, openings and condition rather than cycling identical attack declarations. Familiarity is narrative memory, not a score. A miss can concede position or expose a charge; a successful hit can interrupt, injure or force a change of plan. These consequences must follow the action, not a hidden damage budget. Stop for the player's consequential decisions; never choose their transformation, target, surrender or willingness to accept injury.

## Techniques, transformations and teams

Keep established forms, prerequisites, technique mastery, power factors, method identity and supply limits. A technique multiplier temporarily raises the user's Power Level for that committed technique only. Compare that increased output to the defender for the clash or impact, once; do not also multiply a damage number. It does not upgrade unrelated punches, defence or movement, and expires with the technique. A charged attack can threaten a stronger fighter without making the attacker equally capable throughout the fight. Delivery, protection, dodging, interruption and counterbeams still matter.

Mastery describes reliability, speed of execution, control, mobility, precision and efficient use. Use qualitative buildup appropriate to the method: Spirit Spear is a quick contact attack; a large Spirit Bomb requires prolonged protected gathering and real donated energy; Special Beam Cannon needs a conspicuous concentrated charge, especially at Learned. No 15-second grid, fixed faction turn, countdown, action points, attack damage stat or upkeep bill. Established scene-specific deadlines and demonstrated feats remain history, not universal action timers. Track ordinary story time coherently as events unfold, without padding fights to hit a target duration.

Ordinary physical attacks and unnamed ki attacks remain useful for pressure, positioning, interruption, probing and creating openings. Named techniques have specific methods and vulnerabilities; neither always lands nor automatically invalidates ordinary combat. Forms can be tiring or physically dangerous according to their lore and mastery. Kaioken's bodily strain and the channel's control/takeover risk remain real narrative limits. Exhaustion can prevent transformation; a restorative item helps according to its established effect, not numerical entry fees.

Do not add team Power Levels into a fictional single fighter. Compare individual threats and actual coordination. Allies can split attention, rescue someone, hold an opening or protect a charge, but cannot occupy impossible positions or make an attack automatically connect. A stronger enemy fights back against the team, targets exposed members and uses its own mobility. Splitting the group changes who can help whom; no simultaneous faction accounting is needed.

## Rough sustained form use

Technique preparation and transformation buildup remain narrative, guided by mastery, the method and an actual opening. No fixed activation or attack timetable returns. Rough sustainable form durations are a separate stamina reference, not countdowns or maximum lifespans.

The current player estimates are owned by PLAYER-TECHNIQUES.json and displayed in Current Capability. As of S268 the proposed working estimates are: Kaioken x2 about 3 minutes; Dark Factor Sealed Channel about 5 minutes. These are narrator assessments for a rested fighter sustaining ordinary combat effort, not newly enacted demonstrations or a universal tier formula. They may be revised through play or player review. Uncontrolled has no demonstrated sustainable duration and is not voluntarily managed.

Basis: Kaioken previously demonstrated one moving minute before Yardrat; later dedicated practice brings it to 1882.4 progress, still Proficient, with Mastered Ki Control. A modest few-minute estimate recognises that progress without granting effortless long-term use. Channel previously demonstrated three strenuous moving minutes; later regulation practice and Mastered Ki Control justify a cautious five-minute estimate, limited by conscious containment. No duration is derived by dividing a retired Endurance pool by its old upkeep.

Heavy charged attacks, injury, accumulated fatigue and maintaining compatible boosts together shorten useful sustain. Quiet maintenance or lower output may last longer. Brief release and re-entry do not erase accumulated strain. A restorative treatment changes the actual condition; there is no expiry that defeats restored stamina. Signal mounting strain in the narrative before ordinary voluntary control fails when circumstances permit. Preserve the Channel's existing takeover triggers and unsafe Kaioken pairing. Estimate each NPC/form individually from its own lore, mastery and feats when needed; do not assign Zero's times to everyone.

## Injuries, healing and uncertainty

Track concrete injuries and their consequences in prose: bruising, burns, cuts, fractures, bleeding, organ damage or limb loss as supported by the hit, location and intent. Minor injury means painful but substantially functional; major injury materially impairs fighting or threatens life. Multiple wounds accumulate and affect the actions the body can actually perform. No 25/50/100 damage thresholds, injury roulette, penalty stacks or recovery-charge pools.

Ordinary fighters may rally if their condition permits, without a Second Wind skill or guaranteed reset. Namekian, bio-android, Majin and immortal regeneration retain their established biological differences, survivability and weaknesses. Senzu restores full health under the established lore; Spirit Healing follows the healer's learned capability and viable tissue. Regeneration does not grant an otherwise unestablished immortality. The campaign's true immortality, soul erasure and wish rules remain lore; their former numerical recovery implementation is retired.

Resolve through established circumstances and plausible consequences. No combat simulator, hit-roll engine, mandatory random defence selector or hidden replacement health meter. The table constrains narration without scripting an entire fight in advance. If an important capability is genuinely unrecorded, establish it consistently before relying on it rather than inventing a rescue after the outcome.

Training, cumulative mastery, acquired forms, art, historical feats and current power levels remain intact.

# Approved campaign power scale

Approved 20 October 2026. This is the controlling reader reference for the 24 listed counterpart encounters, separate from Zero’s current acquired abilities. It supersedes earlier trial scales. Numbers are campaign rulings using a consistent Perfect Power Level List profile selection as the primary numerical comparison; a wiki estimate is not canon proof. Anime, manga and alternate counterparts retain separate ordinary bases.

## Calculation rules

**Fusion base = 2 × (A × eligible factor A + B × eligible factor B).** Apply the fusion’s own form to that base afterwards. Both Potara and Metamoran fusion use this rule. Eligible factors include controllable, available, compatible states and actually supplied energy. Fusion-only unlocks do not retroactively enter the participants. Kaioken is a separate multiplicative technique, never a new intrinsic Blue form. Golden Great Ape is already a total ×500 and is not multiplied by Super Saiyan again. The approved Cumber states are alternatives, not an extra Evil stack on every state.

Dance matching concerns emitted power: suppression or an eligible Kaioken state can match it. Matching suppression does not erase the capacity used by the campaign formula. Uncontrolled forms are excluded. Training, donations and temporary effort must be separately recorded, never silently folded into a shared form factor. Standard charged Kamehameha uses the campaign ×2 attack convention where specified; it does not permanently double bodily power.

All source-encounter figures below are distinct from live custom-character readings. Named large-number labels use the short scale. Table labels may round long fractions; [exact machine-readable data](power-scale.json) preserves the calculation values.

## Accepted encounter adaptations

All eight rulings below were approved together on 20 October 2026. These adaptations intentionally change secondary portrayals; they are not claims about the original footage.

1. Retain the existing capacity-sum fusion equation. Accept the participant benchmarks and form table as campaign estimates, including the revised Buu history and separate anime/manga counterparts. Do not adopt literal base × base.

2. Standardise ordinary SS4 ×4,000, Full SS4 ×25,000 and divine Limit Breaker ×500 Million. The opening Xeno/CC comparison uses **Full SS4**, as the selected Xeno profile already distinguishes it; it is not ordinary SS4 secretly receiving a different multiplier. Prison Xeno Vegito gains Limit Breaker as a **fusion-only early unlock**. Individual Xeno fighters gain it only at their later established stage.

3. Rewrite the Prison sequence: after defusion Goku uses KK20 for distraction/evasion rather than trading physical blows with the ape; Fu destroys the moon. Later Xeno Vegito remains fused to intercept the returning group beam and supplies the decisive counter. Solo UI Goku evades/stalls but cannot overpower this Cumber. These are changes to the secondary anime scene, not claims about original footage.

4. Give Kefla the explicitly defined Kale-family Legendary states. Keep Super Broly’s Berserk lineage separate from Z-derived Broly’s Legendary lineage. No standard SS1–3, God, Blue or Golden Ape factor changes.

5. Treat anime Zamasu’s corrupted release as Corrupted Rosé 3 (God ×SS3), and the Spirit Sword as a one-shot supplied attack with battle-residue energy. It destroys the unstable body, not the immortal essence. This does not apply to the manga version.

6. Rewrite the Crimson fusion duel from Blue → Evolution to **base → SS1 Gogeta**. Crimson retains Rosé 1/2/3, a ×2 Full Power effort and a separately estimated ×2 harvested supply. No new intrinsic Full Power Rosé factor or billionfold donation is added.

7. Later Heroes solo Vegeta comparisons that would otherwise require Evolution to equal the much stronger state use his already acquired Controlled Berserk instead. Ordinary UI, Sign and Evolution retain their stated shared factors.

8. Accept explicitly estimated encounter outputs for the Universe Seed, Universe Tree, Dark King combat body, later Broly/Omega and Demigra. These are calibrated opponent/source endpoints with the displayed margins, not measured canon values or new automatic boost rules. Kibito and manga Vegeta source gaps are filled by labelled campaign estimates.

The approximate margins chosen for otherwise unmeasured opponents are **design allocations**. For example, setting Hearts at 80% of Gogeta means choosing a 1.25-to-1 advantage for this campaign; the source does not establish exactly 80%. Every such choice is exposed below. The model is usable under those approved rulings, without pretending the wiki uniquely determines them.

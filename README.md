# Dragon Ball Stories

A game-style home for Zero's Dragon Ball Isekai, inspired by Capsule Corp equipment and Dragon Ball adventure menus.

[Open the companion](https://noxanimusvicta.github.io/Dragon-Ball-Stories/)

## The app

Six screens: main menu, character, story, abilities, inventory and world. An illustrated world, animated selections, keyboard navigation and optional menu sounds make the adventure fun to explore. Includes search, expandable records, a 131-entry journal with the newest moment first, World filters that display only the selected category, a power-level display with exact values and a full-height portrait prepared to cycle through unlocked forms. Forms cycles through Base, Dark Factor Sealed Channel and Uncontrolled Dark Factor; the power dialog calculates their totals from the recorded base. Browsing art does not activate a form. Switch to Appearance history to tap through earlier approved portraits and see the visual progression to the current build, without visible capture dates. Vegeta’s Base and Wrathful artwork is available in his expanded World record. NPC and place archives appear only when approved art exists; future designs remain hidden. Empty archives stay hidden. The one-star Dragon Ball is used for the favicon and home-screen icon. Motion can be paused, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

The app is a published reference. Send character details and artwork through the chat; there are no device-local character edits or uploads. It checks for new app and story releases automatically when opened or resumed, and every minute while visible. Updates preserve your place and wait for open panels to close. It requires an internet connection and does not install an offline service worker. Open the latest version once to activate automatic updates on an older installation.

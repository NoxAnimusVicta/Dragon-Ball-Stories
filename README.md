# Dragon Ball Stories

A game-style home for Zero's Dragon Ball Isekai, inspired by Capsule Corp equipment and Dragon Ball adventure menus.

[Open the companion](https://noxanimusvicta.github.io/Dragon-Ball-Stories/)

## Current state

Story in progress through **S052, Day 45 evening**. Zero is human, 26 years and 6 months old and 5′6″ (167.64cm). After another day of gravity training with Vegeta, he is healed and has measured 174 twice. He has found Krillin finishing instruction in a Conton City practice hall; Krillin has invited him in and his reply is pending. Controlled 25× warm-ups and deliberate 50× movement are established. Flight and moving ki control still need work; no field clearance, signature technique or transformation.

This companion records enacted, character-known facts, plus the approved profile/art and explicitly labelled OOC home-timeline references. Private preparation and future plot ideas remain outside this repository. Unpublished fields are not proof that no decision exists.

## The app

Six screens: main menu, character, story, abilities, inventory and world. An illustrated world, animated selections, keyboard navigation and optional menu sounds make the adventure fun to explore. Includes search, expandable records, a 52-entry journal with the newest moment first, world-category navigation, a current scouter reading and the updated full-height character portrait with a full-art viewer. The one-star Dragon Ball is used for the favicon and home-screen icon. Motion can be paused, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

The app is a published reference. Send character details and artwork through the chat; there are no device-local character edits or uploads. It checks for new app and story releases automatically when opened or resumed, and every minute while visible. Updates preserve your place and wait for open panels to close. It requires an internet connection and does not install an offline service worker. Open the latest version once to activate automatic updates on an older installation.
